Chapter III · Concept Stage

EnlightenThem

  • Concept stage
  • Solo project
  • Painterly · film frames

A firefly that carries its own light is seized by moths who envy that light and mean to burn it as fuel for a sacrifice. What gets it out is not strength — it is the light itself: the firefly lures the moths after it and slips away. The style has left the watercolor picture book behind, darker now and rougher.
